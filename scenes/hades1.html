HADES:

You are a fool.

ORPHEUS:

What other choice is there? Are you not a fool yourself?

Your hair is like mine.

Your eyes are like mine.

The clothes on your body are the same that I wear.

HADES:

I appear in whatever form you can bear. It is an illusion that protects people like you.

ORPHEUS:

There is no difference between us, Hades.
